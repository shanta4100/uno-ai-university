Programs & Admissions

Graduate-level Master's and PhD programs, and research initiatives shaping the future of AI.

Academic Programs

Choose the path that matches your career goals in artificial intelligence.

AI & Machine Learning

Master's & PhD Full-Time / Part-Time On-Campus & Online

Our flagship program covers deep learning, neural networks, natural language processing, and computer vision. Students build production-grade AI systems and conduct original research alongside faculty mentors.

Data Science

Master's & PhD Full-Time / Part-Time On-Campus & Online

Develop expertise in statistical modeling, big data analytics, and data-driven decision making. Graduates are prepared to lead data science teams across industry, government, and research.

AI Ethics

Master's & PhD Full-Time / Part-Time On-Campus & Online

Examine the governance, fairness, transparency, and policy implications of emerging AI technologies. This interdisciplinary program blends technical training with philosophy, law, and public policy.

Robotics

Master's & PhD Full-Time / Part-Time On-Campus & Online

Study autonomous systems, robotic perception, control, and human-robot interaction. Students gain hands-on lab experience building and testing intelligent robotic systems.

Research Initiatives

Faculty and students collaborate on high-impact research across multiple domains.

Responsible AI Lab

Investigating fairness, transparency, and accountability in machine learning systems.

Autonomous Systems Research

Advancing perception, planning, and control for robots and autonomous vehicles.

Applied Data Science Center

Partnering with industry and government to solve real-world data challenges.

Admissions

Everything you need to know to apply to UNO AI University.

1

Requirements

A bachelor's degree (Master's applicants) or master's degree (PhD applicants) in a related field, official transcripts, and letters of recommendation.

2

Application

Submit your online application, statement of purpose, and supporting documents through our admissions portal.

3

Deadlines

Fall admission: May 1. Spring admission: November 1. Rolling admission available for select programs.
