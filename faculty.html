Research Initiatives

Our faculty lead interdisciplinary research labs and centers.

Responsible AI Lab

Investigating fairness, transparency, and accountability in machine learning systems that affect real people's lives.

Autonomous Systems Research

Advancing perception, planning, and control for robots and autonomous vehicles operating in complex environments.

Applied Data Science Center

Partnering with industry and government to solve real-world data challenges using cutting-edge analytics.

Building the next generation of conversational AI and language understanding systems.

Computer Vision Lab

Applying vision-based AI to healthcare diagnostics, security, and autonomous vehicles.

Shaping regulatory frameworks and best practices for responsible AI deployment.
